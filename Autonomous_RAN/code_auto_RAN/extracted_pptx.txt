Problem Statement
Current 5G and Open RAN deployments still rely heavily on predefined policies, static optimization rules, centralized controllers, and human-assisted operations.
The limitations include:
Dynamic traffic patterns exceeding optimization capabilities.
Massive IoT density creating scheduling complexity.
THz communication introducing highly dynamic channel conditions.
Integrated TN-NTN architectures increasing mobility complexity.
Ultra-low latency applications requiring real-time adaptation.
Energy consumption becoming a major operational concern.
Increasing complexity of network slicing and service orchestration.
Traditional optimization methods become computationally infeasible because many RAN optimization problems belong to NP-hard optimization classes.
How can multiple autonomous AI agents collaboratively optimize scheduling, resource allocation, mobility, energy efficiency, security, beam management, and air interface adaptation in real-time while ensuring network stability, scalability, explainability, and trustworthiness in 6G environments?

Proposed Solution
The proposed framework aims to:
Develop a Multi-Agent AI-Native RAN architecture where specialized agents autonomously manage scheduling, radio resource allocation, security, mobility, energy consumption, network slicing, and air-interface adaptation.
The system must learn continuously from network observations, coordinate actions among agents, and optimize both local and global objectives using reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, federated learning, foundation models, and digital twins.
